# Strategy 02 v1.5 — Locked multi-timeframe Scenario 2

## Long

1. The completed 1-hour Heikin-Ashi candle opens below its 1-hour Jaw and
   closes above it.
2. The 1-hour Alligator remains open downward, making this a reversal setup.
3. At that completion time, the latest completed 15-minute Alligator is open
   upward: Lips > Teeth > Jaw.
4. A confirmed 15-minute ZigZag support exists using Depth `18`, Deviation `5`,
   Backstep `3`.
5. Enter at the next 15-minute bar open. Place the stop beyond that 15-minute
   support plus the volatility buffer.

## Short

1. The completed 1-hour Heikin-Ashi candle opens above its 1-hour Jaw and
   closes below it.
2. The 1-hour Alligator remains open upward, making this a reversal setup.
3. At that completion time, the latest completed 15-minute Alligator is open
   downward: Lips < Teeth < Jaw.
4. A confirmed 15-minute ZigZag resistance exists using Depth `18`, Deviation
   `5`, Backstep `3`.
5. Enter at the next 15-minute bar open. Place the stop beyond that 15-minute
   resistance plus the volatility buffer.

The 1-hour chart confirms the reversal. The 15-minute chart confirms current
direction, owns support/resistance, and executes the trade. All information
must be complete before the entry bar opens.
